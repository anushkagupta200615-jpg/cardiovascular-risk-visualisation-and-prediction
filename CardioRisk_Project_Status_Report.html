Cardiovascular Risk Visualization & Prediction

Project Status, Milestone Audit & Team Work Distribution

Generated on October 20, 2026 • Team Lead: Tooba

1. Executive Project Summary

This project addresses Track A of the Multimodal AI Hackathon 2026 by delivering an integrated 3D interactive human heart viewer coupled with machine learning models that predict Overall CAD status alongside localized stenosis in the three critical coronary vessels (LAD, LCX, RCA), backed by transparent SHAP clinical feature attributions.
